About — the studio

A small studio
with a large bench.

In short

Kaafidesigns is a Delhi-and-Bombay-rooted design & content studio handling the full creative spine for some of India's most-watched creators, marketers and founders.

We started as one editor and one designer. We grew into a curated bench of senior craftspeople — and we still pick up the phone ourselves.

What we believe

That the best creative work in India never gets a credit line. That a clean cut beats a clever one. That clients shouldn't have to chase, follow up, or "circle back".

That if our work is doing its job, you sleep better.

Why us

We bring the best creative resources from India — to you.

India quietly powers a huge chunk of the world's content. The editors cutting your favourite reels, the motion artists behind explainer videos you've shared, the designers behind D2C brands you love — a lot of them are here.

We've spent years finding the senior ones — the people who don't pitch on freelance marketplaces, who only take work through trusted studios. We package that bench, route it through proper briefs and quality control, and hand you the output.

You get craft that's normally locked behind agencies five times our size, at the speed of a tight studio that actually answers messages.

"Studio-grade craft. Founder-grade speed."

House rules

Six things we
genuinely believe.

Pinned above every
editor's monitor.
01.
The first 1.5 seconds is the edit.
Everything after the hook is interior decoration. We obsess over openings — because the algorithm does.
02.
A clean cut beats a clever one.
If a transition makes itself the protagonist, we'll cut it. Restraint is a skill, not a shortage of ideas.
03.
Briefs are sacred. Calls are not.
Async-first. Long Looms, short calls. Your meeting calendar shouldn't pay rent for our process.
04.
Deadlines are a feature.
We treat dates like deliverables. If we said Friday, it's Friday — barring an actual emergency. Even then, you'll hear by Wednesday.
05.
The brand is a feeling, not a font.
We start every project by understanding what your audience should feel in three seconds. The rest is just choosing typography to match.
06.
Quietly good is the highest goal.
No splashy reveals, no humblebrags. Just work that consistently lands — and a client roster that keeps renewing.
